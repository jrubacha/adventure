How to play

You explore a cave by typing short commands, then pressing Enter. Use one or two words: a verb and maybe a thing.

Movego north or just n, s, e, w, up, down, in, out
Look aroundlook
Pick up / dropget lamp, drop lamp
What you carryinventory (or i)
Use thingslight lamp, open grate, unlock grate
Your scorescore
Game helphelp, info, and help help for every verb

↑ and ↓ bring back commands you typed earlier. Your game saves itself after every turn, so you can close the laptop and pick it up next class. Save also gives you a file to keep as a backup.

About

This is a browser build of AdventureCCaw1 by Al Whinery, freeware, based on the original Colossal Cave Adventure by Willie Crowther and Don Woods. Source: github.com/jrubacha/adventure
